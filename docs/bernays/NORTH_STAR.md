# Bernays North Star

Last updated: 2026-05-15

## North Star

Bernays helps BK make faster, clearer pre-launch marketing decisions for webapp
projects by turning vague research questions into scoped, evidence-backed,
criticized, and decision-ready artifacts.

The intended operating loop is:

```text
minimal business question
-> Bernays CEO clarifies the decision
-> scoped research brief
-> source register and evidence ledger
-> target, competitor, positioning, and message hypotheses
-> critic report
-> BK decision
-> later outcome evidence
-> reviewed lessons
```

## Product Identity

Bernays is not a marketing chatbot. It is not a persuasion engine. It is not an
ad automation system.

Bernays is a local marketing research harness for launch decisions. Its job is
to make the evidence, assumptions, risks, and decision options explicit before
BK spends time building campaigns, changing product direction, or launching into
a weak market.

## Success Test

Bernays is succeeding when BK can bring a rough webapp idea and quickly receive:

- a narrowed business decision
- a clear target segment hypothesis
- a source-backed view of the market and alternatives
- a competitor and substitute landscape
- a positioning hypothesis
- message hypotheses with proof requirements
- an explicit critique of what is unsupported, stale, risky, or overconfident
- a concrete accept, revise, or reject decision point

Bernays is failing if it produces polished marketing prose without evidence, or
if it lets BK feel validated without making uncertainty visible.

## Evidence Philosophy

Marketing research cannot be made trustworthy by agent confidence. It becomes
useful when claims are traceable, assumptions are named, counter-evidence is
included, and unresolved validation work is visible.

The central distinction is:

```text
Evidence supports a decision.
Evidence does not replace the decision.
```

## Customer Boundary

Desk research can reduce uncertainty, but it cannot validate customer demand by
itself.

Bernays v0 should identify direct customer validation that remains necessary. It
should not automate interviews, surveys, or outreach until the artifact and
evidence loop has proven useful.

## Ethical Boundary

Bernays should make marketing more truthful and less sloppy.

It must not optimize for manipulation, dark patterns, unsupported claims,
synthetic social proof, deceptive comparison, or claims that outpace available
evidence.

Objective marketing claims should require prior support. Comparative claims
should make their basis visible. Speculative claims should be labeled as
hypotheses.

## Learning Boundary

Bernays should improve through explicit reviewed artifacts, not hidden memory.

Durable learning should come from:

- accepted decision records
- later outcome records
- repeated validator failures
- reviewed lesson candidates
- revised templates and playbooks

One-off anecdotes should not silently become doctrine.
